ROBOT ARM BASE V0.1

Purpose
-------
Early mechanical concept for the 6-servo ESP32/PCA9685 desktop arm.
Designed to fit comfortably on a Bambu Lab P1S.

Nominal dimensions
------------------
Base: 160 x 160 x 18 mm
Outer wall: 5 mm
Floor: 4 mm
Center cartridge zone: approximately 56 x 32 mm clear opening, surrounded by support rails.

IMPORTANT
---------
This is deliberately a V0.1 prototype, NOT the final MG996R mount.
The AliExpress MG996R has not arrived yet, so its body, mounting ears,
shaft location, horn, and screw spacing have not been physically verified.

The center is intended to accept a removable servo cartridge that we will
design after measuring the actual MG996R. This avoids reprinting the whole base.

Suggested Bambu Studio starting settings
----------------------------------------
Material: PLA or PLA+
Layer height: 0.20 mm
Walls: 3
Infill: 15-20%
Supports: none expected
Orientation: flat floor on build plate

Do not mount/power the servo from this prototype yet. First verify actual
servo dimensions when the hardware arrives.
